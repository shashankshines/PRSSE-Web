About the Laboratory Training Programs

Our experimental training program provides students with hands-on experience in scientific exploration and experimentation in our laboratories. Once a week, students will be engaged for hands-on experiments in our state-of-the-art laboratory facilities once a week for 2 hours where students will try their hands for the various experiments in Biology, Chemistry, Electronics, Mathematics, Physics and DIY activities. Students can select the batch slot of their choice as per the merit list of the entrance exam. In an academic year students will get 35 sessions in which they can complete more than 40 activities/tasks/experiments. Moreover, students will get the exposure of other outreach programs of the exploratory viz. Exhibition, guest talk, interactions with visiting scientists and educators across the country, science shows, quiz etc.

Programme Details

  • Equipping students with practical skills and knowledge in conducting experiments
  • Providing training in data analysis and problem solving in specially designed laboratories
  • Exposure training for various National/International competitions in science and technology viz. Vidyarthi Vigyaan Manthan, Homi Bhabha Balvaidnyanik Competition, Junior Science Olympiad etc.

What You will Learn

The Mathematics Olympiad Batch programme aims to develop students' problem-solving skills, logical reasoning, and mathematical abilities. The programme also aims to enhance students' critical thinking, creativity, and perseverance. Ultimately, the program outcome is to prepare students for mathematical competitions, as well as for pursuing advanced studies and careers in mathematics-related fields. Sessions will be delivered by the experts from the city and some guest sessions will also be conducted.
