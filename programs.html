Students Program

Students Program Overview

Professor Rajendra Singh Science Exploratory offers enriching programs for students in middle school to high school in grades 5 till 10. Our year-long experiential learning program is specifically designed for students in grades 6 to 9, fostering hands-on learning and practical skills. Additionally, we organize summer programs and camps for students in grades 5 and 10. Our dedicated trainers work closely in well-equipped laboratories, complemented by visiting experts from across the country, to help students develop essential skills. We also provide training programs for teachers, ensuring a comprehensive educational experience for both students and educators.

Year Long After School Program

Programme 1: Laboratory Training Programs

Enrolment Eligibility: Grades 6 to 9.

No. of Students Enrolment for this program in an academic Year: 350

Tentative No. of Sessions in an Academic Year: 35 - 40 (Each of 2 hours per week)

The Laboratory Training Program is designed to develop strong experimental skills and scientific thinking among students. It focuses on hands-on learning, enabling students to perform experiments, analyze data, and approach problems with a research-oriented mindset. The program also nurtures critical thinking, creativity, and science communication skills, preparing students to confidently tackle real-world challenges.

Students participate in weekly sessions conducted in state-of-the-art laboratory facilities. These sessions provide exposure to a wide range of disciplines from their school curriculum, including Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Electronics, and DIY experimental activities, ensuring a holistic scientific experience.

Admission to the program is based on merit through an entrance examination conducted in the month of April whose notification comes out in the month of February. Students studying in Grades 5, 6, and 7 are eligible to appear for the exam and, upon selection, are admitted to Grades 6, 7, and 8 batches respectively for the academic session beginning in July. Students once selected may continue in the program up to Grade 9. In addition to laboratory activities, students gain broader exposure through special workshops, expert talks by visiting scientists, competitions, and outreach programs.

Programme 2: Dr. Homi Bhabha Balvaidnyanik Competition Training Program

Enrolment Eligibility:6th and 9th Std. Students

This batch will prepare the students for Dr. Homi Bhabha Balvaidnyanik competition. It includes theory, practical and project components related to this year-long renowned competition organized by MSTA, Mumbai. The topics related to the curriculum from Physics, Chemistry, Biology are covered through this programme.

No. of Seats: 25

Timeline:

Selection Process:Through Entrance Exam of Laboratory Training Programme as mentioned above

Declaration of Merit List and Admission Process: JMay first week

Session Commencement:June-April (One week summer camp in May/June)

Total Sessions in an academic Session:30 Practical and 30 Theory

Programme 3: International Junior Science Olympiad Training Programme

Enrolment Eligibility:8th and 9th Std. Students

Our Junior Science Olympiad Training programme is designed to help students prepare for the International Junior Science Olympiad competition. Through a combination of hands-on science activities and expert coaching, students will develop the skills and knowledge they need to succeed. Sessions on the topics in science and mathematics will be delivered by the experts from the city and some guest sessions of renowned science educators across the country are also invited to make this programme vibrant.

No. of Seats: 25

Timeline:

Selection Process:Through Entrance Exam of Laboratory Training Programme as mentioned above

Declaration of Merit List and Admission Process: May first week

Session Commencement:June-May (One week summer camp in May and June)

Total Sessions in an academic Session:30 Practical and 80 Theory

Programme 1: Laboratory Training Programs

Enrolment Eligibility: 6th, 7th, 8th and 9th Std.

The laboratory training program aims to equip students with practical skills and knowledge in conducting experiments, data analysis, and problem-solving. The program also aims to enhance students' critical thinking, research, and science communication skills. Ultimately, the program outcome is to prepare students for real-world challenges in their respective fields.

Once a week, students will be engaged for hands-on experiments in our state-of-the-art laboratory facilities where students will try their hands for the various experiments in Biology, Chemistry, Electronics, Mathematics, Physics and DIY activities.

Students are selected through an entrance exam conducted in the month of April on merit basis. Students of 5th, 6th and 7th Std, can appear in the exam who will be admitted in 6th, 7th and 8th Std. batches for the session commencing in July. Enrolled students can continue their affiliation till 9th Std.

Session Commencement: July-April (One session of two hours every week)

Program Outcomes

  • INCREASED SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE: Students will develop a deeper understanding of scientific concepts, theories and principles through experimentation and exploration.
  • IMPROVED CRITICAL THINKING SKILLS: By engaging in scientific inquiry and problem-solving activities, students will develop critical thinking skills.
  • ENHANCED TEAMWORK AND COLLABORATION: Many scientific investigations require teamwork and collaboration. Students will have the opportunity to work in groups to conduct experiments and analyze data.
  • INCREASED INTEREST IN SCIENCE: Students will develop a greater interest in science and consider pursuing further studies or careers in related fields.
  • IMPROVED ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE: Skills and knowledge gained through science exploratory programmes will improve academic performance.
  • SHINING STARS OF THE CITY: Students will be able to showcase their talent in various national and international science and mathematics programmes.
