About Programs

Program Overview

    Professor Rajendra Singh Science exploratory offers after school Programs. We train students from 6th std. to 9th std. for experiential learning through our different regular Programs. Our main programme is to train around 500 budding students of 7th and 8th Std. from different schools of the city by exposing them to experiential learning through our specially designed experiments. Our dedicated staffs/teachers/instructors in the laboratories help them to develop the necessary skills. Some interested students of this program also get the opportunity to enroll for the olympiad Programs.

Our various programs include:

  • After-school Laboratory Training programs: Our laboratory training programs provide students with hands-on experience in scientific research and experimentation. We offer training programs in chemistry, physics, biology, and other emerging current trends in scientific disciplines. Our after-school programs provide students with the opportunity to continue their science education outside of the classroom once a week for 2 hours.
    Eligibility: 7th and 8th Std. Students
  • Science camps: Our science camps during winter and summer vacations are a great way for students to immerse themselves in the world of science and explore a variety of scientific disciplines in a fun and engaging way. Our camps are designed for 5th and 6th grade students. Notifications for the admissions for these camps are published in the month of April.
    Eligibility: 5th and 6th Std. Students
  • STEM workshops: Our STEM workshops are designed to provide students with hands-on experience in science, technology, engineering, and math. We offer workshops on topics such as rocketry, 3D printing, and renewable energy.Notifications for the admissions for these camps are published in the month of April.
    Eligibility: 9th and 10th Std. students
  • Science fairs, demonstration shows and competitions: We host a variety of science fairs and competitions throughout the year, giving students the opportunity to showcase their scientific projects and compete against their peers.
    Eligibility: 6th to 9th Std. students
  • Field trips and excursions: Our field trips and excursions provide students with the opportunity to explore the world of science outside of the classroom. We offer trips to museums, science centers, and other scientific institutions.
    Eligibility: Students of Olympiad Training Batch
  • Tutoring and mentoring for Mathematics and Junior Science Olympiad programme.
    Eligibility: Selected students of 8th Std.
  • Exposure camps for the second phase of Dr Homi Bhabha Competition and Vidyarthi Vigyaan Manthan (VVM): The exposure is an excellent opportunity for qualified students to enhance their knowledge and skills in science, and prepare them for the second phase of Dr Homi Bhabha Competition.
    Eligibility: Qualified students of the first phase of the competition
