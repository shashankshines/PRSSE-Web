At Prof. Rajendra Singh Science Exploratory (PRSSE), we believe science is more than just a subject - it's a way of life. Our mission is to inspire and educate young minds to appreciate science, and to help them explore the world around them in new and exciting ways. Established in 2015, the exploratory is designed to provide students hands-on experience in a wide range of scientific disciplines. From Chemistry to Physics, Biology to Astronomy and Mathematics to Design Thinking, we offer a variety of Programs and activities for the students of 6th Std. to 9th Std. Our experienced and dedicated team of advisors and experts across the country is committed to providing an engaging learning environment to students. Whether you're a budding scientist or just curious about the world around you, we have something for everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions:

The Professor Rajendra Singh Science Exploratory (PRSSE), established in 2015, is an educational institution run by an NGO, focused on inspiring and educating young minds through hands-on scientific exploration. It is located in west Nagpur near Alankar Talkies near Shankar Nagar Metro Station.

The Laboratory Training Program offers students practical experience in various scientific disciplines, including Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics, through hands-on experiments conducted in state-of-the-art laboratories. Students visit once a week for two hours for this year-long programme.
Key features of the Laboratory Training Program
  • Hands-on experiments in various scientific fields
  • Around 35 sessions throughout the academic year.
  • Exposure to national/international competitions.
  • Outreach programs, including exhibitions, sky watching and guest talks.

A total of 400 students will be selected from various schools of the city through entrance exams scheduled in April for the year-long training program.

Students currently in 5th, 6th, and 7th grades in Nagpur are eligible to register for the Entrance Exam.

Date of Entrance Exam: 3rd April, 4th April, 5th April, 11th April, 12th April, and 14th April 2026.
Holidays and Weekends in April.

The Entrance Exam will be conducted on April 10, 12, 13, 14, and 18, 2025 at various slots. Students can select the slot of their choice in the registration form. Students need to appear for exams in the laboratories at PRSSE.

The Entrance Exam fee is ₹50, which can be paid online via UPI.

The exam will assess theoretical knowledge, observational skills, and motor skills based on the middle school syllabus of Maharashtra State Board and CBSE.

Annual Fees: ₹ 6500/-
50% concession available for the students from economically weaker sections.
