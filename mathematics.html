Mathematics Olympiad Training

  • No. of Seats: 30
  • Duration: August 2023 - July 2024
  • No. of contact sessions during the academic year: 60
  • Fees: Rs. 10000/-
  • Eligibility: 8th Std. (Only for the selected students of Experimental Training Programme)
  • Timings: Saturday Evening hours and Sunday Morning hours (3 hours per week)

What You will Learn

The Mathematics Olympiad Batch programme aims to develop students' problem-solving skills, logical reasoning, and mathematical abilities. The programme also aims to enhance students' critical thinking, creativity, and perseverance. Ultimately, the program outcome is to prepare students for mathematical competitions, as well as for pursuing advanced studies and careers in mathematics-related fields. Sessions will be delivered by the experts from the city and some guest sessions will also be conducted.

Admission Open

Admission to Prof. Rajendra Singh Exploratory is open to all students who have an interest in science. Admissions are fixed in the month of June-July for one year academic session till April.
