Programme Outcomes

  • INCREASED SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE: Students will develop a deeper understanding of scientific concepts, theories and principles through experimentation and exploration.
  • IMPROVED CRITICAL THINKING SKILLS: By engaging in scientific inquiry and problem-solving activities, students will develop critical thinking skills.
  • ENHANCED TEAMWORK AND COLLABORATION: Many scientific investigations require teamwork and collaboration. Students will have the opportunity to work in groups to conduct experiments and analyze data.
  • INCREASED INTEREST IN SCIENCE: Students will develop a greater interest in science and consider pursuing further studies or careers in related fields.
  • IMPROVED ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE: Skills and knowledge gained through science exploratory Programs will improve academic performance.
  • SHINING STARS OF THE CITY: Students will be able to showcase their talent in various national and international science and mathematics Programs.
