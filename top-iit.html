Institutes of India

Top 10 Indian Research Institutes

  • Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru - This is a premier research institute in India, known for its research in areas such as science, engineering, and technology.
  • Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Mumbai - This is a research institute that focuses on fundamental research in areas such as physics, mathematics, and biology.
  • Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Bombay - This is a premier engineering institute in India, known for its research in areas such as computer science, electrical engineering, and mechanical engineering.
  • Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Delhi - This is another premier engineering institute in India, known for its research in areas such as computer science, electrical engineering, and mechanical engineering.
  • Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata - This is a research institute that focuses on statistics and mathematics, and is known for its contributions to the field of statistics.
  • Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research (JNCASR), Bengaluru - This is a research institute that focuses on interdisciplinary research in areas such as physics, chemistry, and biology.
  • National Centre for Biological Sciences (NCBS), Bengaluru - This is a research institute that focuses on biological research, and is known for its contributions to the field of neuroscience.
  • Harish-Chandra Research Institute (HRI), Allahabad - This is a research institute that focuses on mathematics and theoretical physics, and is known for its contributions to the field of algebraic geometry.
  • Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune - This is a group of research institutes that focus on interdisciplinary research in areas such as physics, chemistry, and biology.
  • Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) - This is a network of 38 research institutes across India that focus on various areas of science and technology, including chemistry, biology, physics, and engineering.
