Great Minds who Changed the World

List of some great scientists along with their date of births and field of work:

  • Isaac Newton (1643) - Physics, Mathematics, Astronomy
  • Albert Einstein (1879) - Physics
  • Marie Curie (1867) - Physics, Chemistry
  • Charles Darwin (1809) - Biology, Natural History
  • Galileo Galilei (1564) - Physics, Astronomy
  • Nikola Tesla (1856) - Electrical Engineering, Physics
  • Stephen Hawking (1942) - Physics, Cosmology
  • Richard Feynman (1918) - Physics
  • Louis Pasteur (1822) - Microbiology, Immunology
  • James Clerk Maxwell (1831) - Physics, Mathematics
