About Programme

Join the Diwali Vacations Hands-on Training at Professor Rajendra Singh Science Exploratory!

Calling all 6th-8th Std Students! Get ready for an exciting opportunity to explore the fascinating world of science through one day hands-on experiments on Physics, Chemistry, and Electronics.

Select any Date of your choice from the following dates: (For 6th - 18th to 20thNovember, for 7th & 8th - 21st to 22nd November 2023)

Participation certificate for all attendees
