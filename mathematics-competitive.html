What You will Learn

Are you ready to take your mathematical skills to the next level? Join the Mathematics Competitive exam Cell at Professor Rajendra Singh Exploratory and embark on a journey of exploration, learning, and competition. Our program is designed to enhance your mathematical abilities and prepare you for national-level competitions like IOQM, RMO, NMTC etc. The students will be selected based on an online entrance exam and personal interview and will be trained for vigorous content for 10 months. The registration process for the online entrance exam is currently underway.

Expected Outcome of the programme:

  • Strengthened Mathematical Foundation: Develop a strong foundation in mathematics, gaining a deeper understanding of various concepts and their applications.
  • Enhanced Problem-Solving Skills: Sharpen your problem-solving skills, critical thinking abilities, and logical reasoning through regular practice.
  • Competitive Edge: Acquire the knowledge and skills necessary to excel in national-level mathematics competitions, giving you a competitive edge among your peers.
  • Confidence Boost: Progress through the sessions, successfully tackling challenging problems, and witness a significant boost in your confidence.
  • Exposure to Advanced Topics: The curriculum of Mathematics Olympiads covers advanced topics like Number Theory, Combinatorics etc. in Mathematics that go beyond standard curriculum. This exposure can be beneficial when students encounter similar topics in the examinations like IIT JEE and above.
