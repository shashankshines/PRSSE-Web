Exploratory Perks

Why is Science Exploratory different from a classroom programme?

Joining a science exploratory can be an exciting and rewarding experience! Here are some reasons to join an exploratory

  • HANDS-ON LEARNING: A science exploratory offers hands-on experience. It allows experimentation and exploration using specialized equipment. Something a classroom programme does not offer. Our mission is to inspire and educate young minds to appreciate science, and to help them explore the world around them in new and exciting ways.

  • EXPERT INSTRUCTION: A science exploratory is mentored by professionals with expertise in various scientific fields. This means students will have access to expert instruction and guidance.

  • COLLABORATIVE LEARNING: A science exploratory emphasizes on group work and collaboration, creating a team and developing valuable communication skills of students.

  • PREPARATION FOR COMPETITIVE EXAMS AND PROJECTS: For students interested in pursuing a career in science, joining an exploratory is a great way to gain experience by appearing in competitive exams and participating in science and mathematics projects.

  • FUN AND ENGAGING: A science exploratory is fun and engaging. It provides a stimulating and challenging environment, creating lifelong love of science.

Special Perks at the New Location of the Exploratory:

  • Budding students in the city can have the opportunity to access state-of-the-art scientific equipment, including some International Olympiad setups.
  • New venue is well-connected to metro and bus services in West Nagpur (Near Shankar Nagar Metro Station).
  • Exploratory can serve as a meeting point for merit students from various schools in the city.
  • Students can receive mentorship and guidance from experienced professionals, which can help them achieve their academic and career goals.
  • Students can get the adequate guidance for competitive exams in science and mathematics, including Junior Science and Mathematics Olympiad Programs, Dr Homi Bhabha Balvaidnyanik Competition, National Mathematics talent Competition (NMTC), Vidyarthi Vigyaan Manthan (VVM), Inspire Camps, etc. through different programs and workshops by the exploratory.
  • Students can participate in science demonstration shows and STEM workshops delivered by invited science educators, scientists and experts across the country.
  • Students from economically weaker sections of society are eligible for annual fee waiver for the regular Programs of the exploratory.
  • Students can be eligible for a certificate of appreciation upon completion of the experimental training programme through a skill test.

Admission Open

Admission to Prof. Rajendra Singh Exploratory is open to all students who have an interest in science. Admissions are fixed in the month of June-July for one year academic session till April.
