Exploratory Perks

Why Choose a Science Exploratory Over a Classroom Programme?

Joining a science exploratory can be an exciting and rewarding experience. It offers opportunities to learn through experimentation, collaboration, mentorship, and scientific exploration beyond traditional classroom learning.

Experience science beyond textbooks.

Hands-on Learning

A science exploratory offers hands-on experience. It allows experimentation and exploration using specialized equipment. Something a classroom programme does not offer. Our mission is to inspire and educate young minds to appreciate science, and to help them explore the world around them in new and exciting ways.

Expert Instruction

A science exploratory is mentored by professionals with expertise in various scientific fields. This means students will have access to expert instruction and guidance, enhancing their scientific knowledge and skills.

Collaborative Learning

A science exploratory emphasizes on group work and collaboration, creating a team and developing valuable communication skills of students, while fostering leadership, teamwork, confidence, creativity and problem-solving abilities.

Preparation for Competitive Exams and Projects

For students interested in pursuing a career in science, joining an exploratory is a great way to gain experience by appearing in competitive exams and participating in science and mathematics projects.

Fun and Engaging

A science exploratory is fun and engaging. It provides a stimulating and challenging environment, creating lifelong love of science, while encouraging curiosity, innovation, exploration, discovery, and meaningful learning experiences.

Special Perks at the New Location.

State-of-the-Art Scientific Equipment

Budding students in the city can have the opportunity to access state-of-the-art scientific equipment, including some International Olympiad setups.

Excellent Connectivity

New venue is well-connected to metro and bus services in West Nagpur (Near Shankar Nagar Metro Station).

Meeting Point for Merit Students

Exploratory can serve as a meeting point for merit students from various schools in the city.

Professional Mentorship

Students can receive mentorship and guidance from experienced professionals, which can help them achieve their academic and career goals.

Competitive Exam Guidance

Students can get the adequate guidance for competitive exams in science and mathematics, including Junior Science and Mathematics Olympiad Programmes, Dr Homi Bhabha Balvaidnyanik Competition, NMTC, VVM, Inspire Camps, etc.

National STEM Exposure

Students can participate in science demonstration shows and STEM workshops delivered by invited science educators, scientists and experts across the country.

Fee Waiver Support

Students from economically weaker sections of society are eligible for annual fee waiver for the regular Programs of the exploratory.

Certification Opportunity

Students can be eligible for a certificate of appreciation upon completion of the experimental training programme through a skill test.
