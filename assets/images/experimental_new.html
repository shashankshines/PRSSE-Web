Hands-On Learning: The Educational Approach at PRSSE

At the Professor Rajendra Singh Science Exploratory (PRSSE), we believe that science is more than just a subject—it's a way of life. Our mission is to inspire and educate young minds to appreciate science and explore the world in exciting ways. Established in 2015, PRSSE provides hands-on experience across various scientific disciplines, including Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Astronomy, Mathematics, and Design Thinking. Unlike traditional experiments, which often focus on predefined outcomes, our exploratory approach encourages students to think critically, ask questions, and engage in open-ended investigations. This fosters a deeper understanding of scientific concepts and cultivates a spirit of inquiry. Our dedicated team of trainers, advisors and experts is committed to creating an engaging learning environment for all students. Whether you're a budding scientist or simply curious, we have something for everyone! Over the years, students enrolled in various programs offered by the exploratory have achieved significant success in embracing science, paving the way for their future aspirations.

About the Laboratory Training Programme

Our Laboratory Training Programme offers valuable hands-on experience in scientific exploration and experimentation. Each week, students will engage in experiments in our cutting-edge laboratory facilities for 2 hours at PRSSE. Participants will explore various fields, including Biology, Chemistry, Electronics, Mathematics, Physics, and DIY activities.

  • Batch Selection:

    Students can choose their preferred batch slot based on the merit list of our entrance exam on a first come first serve basis.

  • Sessions:

    Throughout the academic year, students can attend around 35 sessions, completing over 40 activities/tasks/and experiments including some mini projects.

  • Outreach Programmes:

    Students will benefit from various outreach initiatives, such as exhibitions, guest talks, interactions with visiting scientists and educators, science shows, quizzes, sky watching, webinars and more.




Highlights of the Laboratory Training Programme:

  • Development of practical skills and knowledge in conducting experiments
  • Training in data analysis and problem-solving
  • Exposure to various national/international competitions in science and technology (e.g., Vidyarthi Vigyaan Manthan, Homi Bhabha Balvaidnyanik Competition, Science Olympiads)
  • Exposure to telescope handling sessions during astronomical events.
  • Exposure to National/International platforms to represent innovation.

The Professor Rajendra Singh Science Exploratory (PRSSE), established in 2015, is an educational institution run by an NGO, focused on inspiring and educating young minds through hands-on scientific exploration. It is located in west Nagpur near Alankar Talkies near Shankar Nagar Metro Station.

The Laboratory Training Program offers students practical experience in various scientific disciplines, including Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics, through hands-on experiments conducted in state-of-the-art laboratories. Students visit once a week for two hours for this year-long programme.
Key features of the Laboratory Training Program
  • Hands-on experiments in various scientific fields
  • Around 35 sessions throughout the academic year.
  • Exposure to national/international competitions.
  • Outreach programs, including exhibitions, sky watching and guest talks.
